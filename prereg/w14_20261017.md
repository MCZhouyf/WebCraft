## Reasoning twice and discrepancy policy

Pass 1 separates measurement, supplied evidence, retrieval and policy causes.
Condition-invariant CDS success alone does not establish invariant evidence;
catalogue membership alone does not establish target-world invalidity. Audit
both, and trace the actual data flow before attributing a cause.
Pass 2 checks denominators, target-versus-comparison-world labels, support in
both worlds, negative facts, runtime information isolation and alternative
routes. Do not infer that a positive recall ceiling must improve SR, or that
relaxing a posterior threshold necessarily creates informative observations.

Scientific specification mismatches do not block unrelated work: record them,
use the conservative conventions below, or mark not_reported. Never tune rules
to held-out outcomes. Safety failures (lock, resources, hidden runtime reads,
or frozen-file changes) require a safe stop. No passwords or endpoint values
are read, printed or committed. An encrypted archive can be inspected via its
packaging source/manifest and cryptographic digest without decrypting it.
